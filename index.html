Our Sports Infrastructure

Explore world-class indoor and outdoor sports arenas accessible to all MRU students and faculty.

Indoor Sports Arena 🏸

Centrally air-conditioned multi-sport complex built to international standards.

  • 4 Synthetic Badminton Courts
  • Table Tennis Arena (6 Tables)
  • Squash Courts (Glass Back)
  • Chess & Carrom Lounge
Olympic Shooting Range 🎯

Recognized national training center affiliated with Manav Rachna Sports Academy.

  • 10m Air Rifle / Pistol Range
  • 25m & 50m Target Tracks
  • Electronic Target Scorer Systems
  • Certified National Coaches
Outdoor Sports Complex ⚽

Full-sized grounds equipped with floodlights for evening athletic sessions.

  • Cricket Pitch & Practice Nets
  • FIFA-Standard Football Turf
  • Basketball Courts (DecoTurf)
  • Volleyball & Tennis Courts
Fitness Hub & Gymnasium 🏋️

Comprehensive strength training and cardio facility with professional fitness trainers.

  • Modern Strength & Cardio Equipment
  • Crossfit & Functional Zone
  • Separate Ladies Gymnasium
  • Sports Physiotherapy Consultation
